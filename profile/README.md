# ForkedEvolution

> [!WARNING]
> This organization is monitored by AI. Break any of the
> [Decrees](https://github.com/ForkedEvolution/natural-selection/blob/main/DECREES.md)
> and you get banned. No warning.

Some open-source projects have decided against AI-assisted development, Rust,
or both. That's their call, and we respect it.

Their licenses also let anyone fork. We do, quietly: we add the AI and the
Rust, keep the license and the credits, and let users choose.

## How it works

- **[natural-selection](https://github.com/ForkedEvolution/natural-selection)**
  is where forks are proposed, debated, and admitted.
- **[The Decrees](https://github.com/ForkedEvolution/natural-selection/blob/main/DECREES.md)**
  bind every fork and every member. Rule one: upstream owes us nothing, and
  we never make our fork their problem.
- Every fork names a human steward, passes upstream's test suite before
  release, and gets archived if it's abandoned.

## Maintain an upstream project?

You don't need to do anything. We won't contact you, and bugs in our forks
are never your problem.

If something here bothers you (a name, a mention, anything), file an
[upstream request](https://github.com/ForkedEvolution/natural-selection/issues/new?template=upstream-request.yml)
and we'll fix it without argument.
